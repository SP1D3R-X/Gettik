✂️ Feature #4

Smart Video Auto Split Engine

Intelligent splitting system designed for short-form platforms. Videos 35 seconds or shorter are left untouched. Videos longer than 35s can be automatically split into equal parts.

STRICT SPLIT ENGINE RULE:
1 video + 3 parts = 3 final output files (not 4)

When Auto Split is enabled, ONLY split output parts are saved. The original full video is used temporarily for processing and automatically removed so your disk stays clean.

5. Smart Download Cache

Database-backed persistent cache system. Videos are indexed by platform + video ID. Avoids redundant metadata requests and automatically skips previously completed downloads unless explicit Re-Download is selected.

🔄

6. Creator Sync Engine

Saves creator metadata without keeping bloated video files in RAM. Incremental pagination prevents RAM spikes. Weekly sync checks saved creators for new videos without re-downloading old history.

⚙️

7. 10-Worker Concurrency

Locked at exactly 10 concurrent workers for optimal stability. Concurrency locking prevents double downloads. Auto-retry support up to 5 total attempts across Pending, Processing, Retrying, Completed, and Failed states.

⚡

8. Stream-Optimized Architecture

Chunk-based streaming download pipeline, efficient network usage, and lightweight queue management. Engineered specifically to run smoothly even on lower-RAM PCs.
